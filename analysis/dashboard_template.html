3 · Fair value

Which listings ask less than similar homes nearby

The model estimates each home's price from its size, bathrooms, age, parking, type (condo or townhouse), tenure, city, neighbourhood and walking distance to SkyTrain. Each home is valued without its own price in the data, so a listing can't pull its own estimate toward itself. Ringed dots ask at least 10% below that estimate.

How luxurious is it?

A finish score from 0 to 10, built from the building's amenities (concierge, pool, gym, sauna, guest suite) and the listing's own words for finishes (premium appliances, stone counters, air conditioning, heated floors, views, penthouse). Tiers: Standard under 2, Upgraded 2–4, Premium 4–6.5, Luxury 6.5 and up.

Schools: how good is the elementary school?

School rating out of 10: where the school's Grade 4 and 7 Foundation Skills Assessment (FSA) results for 2023–2026 rank among Metro Vancouver public schools. 10 means top 10% and 5 means middle of the pack. The school is the listing's catchment school when the listing names one, otherwise the nearest public elementary (confirm the catchment with the school district).

Walkability: what can you do without a car?

Walk score 0–100

Built the way Walk Score works, from OpenStreetMap: groceries, restaurants and cafes, shops, errands and health services, parks and playgrounds, rec centres and libraries, childcare, bus stops and SkyTrain. Anything within 300 m (about 4 minutes) counts fully, and credit fades to nothing by 2 km.

5 · Shortlist

Candidates for the family

Sorted by how far each home asks below its model value. Warnings flag the usual reasons a price looks low, such as leasehold land, a presale, or a third bedroom that is really a den. Tap a row for its schools, SkyTrain station, price history and the listing link.

Monthly cost assumptions

Monthly cost = mortgage + strata fee + property tax ÷ 12. The rate is a placeholder, so enter the one your lender quotes. If a listing is missing its fee or tax, the page uses the median for that city and type and marks it “est.”

Scorecards

Every home gets a player card. The six attributes are rated 40–99: VAL asking price against model value, SPC floor area, SCH school rating and distance, WLK walk score, LOC location premium, BLD building age and finish. The overall rating (OVR) weights them with the sliders below. Card colour: gold for 80+, silver for 70–79, bronze below that, and a dark Value pick card for clean listings that ask 15% or more below model value. Tap a card to open the listing.
